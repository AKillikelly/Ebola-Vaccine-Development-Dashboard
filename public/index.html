Stage assignment

Rows are mapped to the highest documented target-specific milestone, not to platform maturity. Example: Ervebo is stockpiled for Zaire, while the same rVSV platform applied to Bundibugyo remains clinical-enabling.

Species-specific claims

Zaire, Sudan, Taï Forest, and Bundibugyo are treated as human-disease species. Reston and Bombali are shown as no-human-disease/gap lanes unless new evidence changes prioritization.

Current-status caveat

Regulatory and trial status can change quickly during outbreaks. The generated CSV includes source URLs, automation method, last-checked timestamp, and supporting-record count for row-wise audit before operational, procurement, or investment decisions.

Automated sources include ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA/CDC/openFDA, EMA product pages, WHO/CEPI/sponsor watch pages, EBOVAC, and GDELT discovery signals. This dashboard is an automated landscape view, not a substitute for regulatory advice or a complete clinical-trial registry.
